Built for people who’d rather be on the water

Wingfoiling, kitesurfing, windsurfing, sailing, kitefoiling, parawing — if you care about wind, gusts, direction, waves, and a real session window, you’re who we had in mind.

Classic wind apps excel at charts. You still reconcile models, guess if offshore is rideable for your sport, and wonder whether two green hours count as a session — and even when the numbers look fine, the spot might not be: closed parking, flooded access, sketchy launch for a kite, algae, or nobody on the water when social says otherwise.

Spot intelligence (rolling out with the community) is meant to close that gap — live station readings and today’s curve, plus live cams, recent posts from the launch, photos, parking and water-level notes, and launchability for your sport — so you get a clearer go / no-go before you drive, not just a green wind cell.

Windmate is opinionated in a helpful way: rideable windows, ranked spots (favorites and newcomers in range), model agreement in color, and stickers from Nice session to Unsure when the hours won’t form a real window. On session day, today’s curve plots the forecast against the nearest live meteo station so you can see whether the model’s holding before you pack the car.

Tech underneath, mate tone on top — honesty when data’s thin, not false hype.

  1. Set your rules

    Sports, min wind, max gust, temps, offshore policy — saved per sport.

Horizon Planner

Seven days at a glance: wind and gust ranges, rideable hours, shared windows across spots. Tap a day to re-rank. Toggle Only rideable hours when you want less noise.

Watching & stickers

Favorites up front with excitement tiers and session stickers. On the day of the session, open today’s curve: actual wind and gusts from the closest live meteo station on the same chart as forecast — mate-tone readouts when they line up or drift. Unsure when rideable hours don’t meet your minimum window.

Departure planner

Windmate finds the best rideable window that satisfies your minimum session length (same logic as ranking), then lines up travel: leave-by or go now hints, estimated drive time, on-the-water hours, and Open in Google Maps aimed at your target arrival — on Watching cards and ranked spot rows.

Spot discovery & ranking

Not just your usual launches. The matrix ranks every rideable spot in range — badges like “most good hours” and “longest window” — so a great day somewhere new can outrank a tired favorite. Search and star what you want in Watching; the planner still surfaces the best match for that day.

Coming together · spot intelligence

Local intel — go / no-go before you drive

Wind and waves are only half the story. Spot intelligence pulls together what’s happening at the launch — spot photos and media, live cams, social posts from riders on site, plus parking, access, and water-condition signals as we wire them up — into a plain go / no-go read alongside your forecast. When ground truth says skip it, we rank the spot down and say why in mate tone. The goal: no more drive-and-cry at arrival — forecast looked rideable, ground truth wasn’t. Some pieces are live today; the rest is rolling out in the open with the community.

Rideability Matrix

Hour-by-hour Beaufort wind and gust, waves, direction, probability, and score — inset spot map, mate-tone notes when a window is borderline, and solid blocks for your longest shared window across models. On today’s session, a live strip from the nearest station sits on the card too — same confidence idea as the curve on Watching.

Legend
